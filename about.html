Our Story

A professionally led practice built on communication and care.

Language and Hearing Solutions (Pty) Ltd was established in 2024 as a professionally led healthcare practice providing audiology and speech therapy services. The practice supports individuals, families, corporates, industrial clients and rehabilitation-related cases across Gauteng, Rustenburg, Witbank and surrounding areas.

Language and Hearing Solutions is a single-director, professionally led healthcare practice, registered with the Health Professions Council of South Africa (HPCSA) and compliant with CIPC and SARS requirements.
