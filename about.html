About the practice

Professional care, centred on connection.

Language and Hearing Solutions (Pty) Ltd was established in 2024 as a professionally led South African healthcare practice providing audiology and speech therapy services.

Our story

A practice designed to make the next step clearer.

The practice is a single-director healthcare practice supporting individuals, families, corporates, industrial clients and rehabilitation-related cases across Gauteng, Rustenburg, Witbank and surrounding areas.

Our work includes assessment, diagnosis, management, rehabilitation, workplace screening and relevant professional reporting. We aim to make care understandable, respectful and accessible.

Our clinical approach

Warmth and professional boundaries can coexist.

The practice works across patient-facing, rehabilitation and workplace environments. Services are shaped around the presenting need, with appropriate assessment, management, recommendations and referral.
