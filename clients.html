Clients & Case Studies

Our work in the field

Verified examples from workplace screening visits, showing the real-world value of accessible hearing screening and early referral.

These figures relate to specific workplace screening visits only and do not represent the total number of patients served by the practice.

Sasol Mine Secunda

25People screened
15Required ear-wax management
11Failed screening due to wax blockage and were referred for further assessment

The screening highlighted the importance of identifying and managing potentially reversible causes of failed hearing screening, with appropriate referrals for further assessment.

Transnet

40People screened
11Required wax management
2Presented possible hearing loss
4Had tympanic membrane perforations

Several people screened also reported itchy ears. Screening identified multiple ear-health concerns requiring management and/or further clinical assessment.

These examples demonstrate the value of accessible screening and early referral in workplace and community environments.
