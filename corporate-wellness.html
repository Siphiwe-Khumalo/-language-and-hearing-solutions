A practical workplace partner

Support for screening, early identification and the next step.

Workplace visits can make hearing screening more accessible to employees while helping organisations identify concerns, support referral and plan appropriate follow-up.

Services are discussed and shaped around the organisation's needs. We do not make unsupported legal or compliance promises; the focus is professional screening, clear communication and responsible referral.

Baseline Audiograms

Establishing a reference point for employees' hearing at the start of employment or exposure.
