Onsite Services

Services for your organisation

Onsite Hearing Screening

Hearing screening conducted at your workplace, minimising downtime for staff.

Baseline Audiograms

Establishing a reference point for employees' hearing at the start of employment or exposure.

Periodic Audiograms

Ongoing monitoring of hearing over time for employees exposed to occupational noise.

Exit Audiograms

Hearing assessment conducted at the end of employment or exposure period.

COID-Aligned Assessments

Assessments aligned with Compensation for Occupational Injuries and Diseases considerations.

Hearing Conservation Programmes

Structured support for workplaces exposed to occupational noise.

Aural Rehabilitation

Support for employees managing hearing difficulties identified through screening.

Professional Referrals

Appropriate referral pathways for employees requiring further assessment or care.

Workplace Wellness Initiatives

Screening days and wellness campaigns tailored to your organisation's needs.
