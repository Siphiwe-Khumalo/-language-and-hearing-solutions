Our Services

Audiology

Hearing and balance services

Professional assessment, management and support for hearing and balance-related needs, for individuals and workplaces.

Newborn Hearing Screening

Early identification of potential hearing difficulties.

Diagnostic Hearing Assessment

Comprehensive assessment to understand hearing ability and identify potential hearing loss.

Industrial Audiology

Workplace hearing screening and occupational hearing services.

Hearing Conservation Programmes

Support for workplaces exposed to occupational noise.
