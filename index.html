About Us

Care that listens. Expertise that makes a difference.

Language and Hearing Solutions is a professionally led South African practice specialising in the assessment, diagnosis and management of hearing disorders, balance disorders, speech disorders and language disorders. Services are available to individuals as well as corporate, industrial, rehabilitation and trust-fund clients.
