A practice built around listening

Care that listens. Expertise that makes a difference.

Language and Hearing Solutions is a South African practice specialising in the assessment, diagnosis and management of hearing disorders, balance disorders, speech disorders and language disorders.

Our work is centred on clear communication, professional evaluation and practical support for the individual, the family and the organisation.

  • Professionally led, HPCSA registered practice
  • Support across Gauteng, Rustenburg and Witbank
  • Individual, family, rehabilitation and corporate services
